## 1. Executive Summary

This report evaluates historical retail performance across key product categories including **bed_bath_table**, **garden_tools**, **health_beauty**, **computers_accessories**, **watches_gifts**, **furniture_decor**, **cool_stuff**, **consoles_games**, and **perfumery**. 

The portfolio demonstrates distinct performance clusters:
- **Top Revenue Drivers:** Specific high-performing SKUs in *watches_gifts* (`watches6`), *bed_bath_table* (`bed5`), *computers_accessories* (`computers6`), and *health_beauty* (`health2`) generate substantial single-month revenues, frequently exceeding 5,000.00 to 12,000.00.
- **Volume Hubs:** Peak seasonal demand occurs around late Q4 (November–December) and Q1/Q2 promotional windows, with items like `furniture2` reaching 122 units in a month and `watches6` achieving 114 units.
- **Pricing Polarization:** Significant divergence exists across pricing flags. Items flagged as **Potentially High** (e.g., `health5` with variance percentages up to 570.0619745965515%) frequently sustain strong sales volume, while items classified as **Revenue Opportunity** with **Potentially Low** flags (e.g., `bed1`, `health9`, `watches3`) show steady volume at deeply discounted relative price levels.
- **Strategic Focus:** Immediate operational reviews should target low performers burdened by high-price flags (`garden3`, `garden5`), volume drop-offs in previously active SKUs (`cool3`, `consoles1`), and monetization reviews for underpriced high-demand products.

## 7. Business Recommendations

### Areas to Investigate
1. **Margin Recapture on "Revenue Opportunity" SKUs:**
   - Investigate price elasticity for `bed1`, `watches3`, and `health10`. These products maintain solid transaction volumes despite large negative variance percentages (-20% to -60%).
   - Review competitor pricing benchmarks to determine if discounts are driving essential volume or unnecessarily eroding margin.

2. **Demand Destruction in "Low Performer" SKUs with "Potentially High" Flags:**
   - Audit `garden3` and `garden5`. Both items experienced steep volume declines (collapsing to 1–2 units monthly) as their price variances exceeded +80% to +140%. 
   - Analyze whether aggressive price increases directly choked off conversion rates.

3. **Post-Surge Drop-Offs:**
   - Investigate the root cause behind sharp volume drop-offs for `cool3` (from 76 units in 01-2018 to 1 unit in 08-2018) and `computers5` (from 57 units in 01-2018 to 3 units in 07-2018). Determine whether these were inventory stock-outs, post-holiday demand cliffs, or lost Buy Box status.

### Areas to Monitor
1. **Premium Positioning Sustainability for Top Health Performers:**
   - Closely monitor customer satisfaction, return rates, and competitor entry for `health2` and `health5`. Both generate large revenue shares while operating at extreme positive variance levels (+450% to +570%).
2. **Q4 Seasonal Preparation:**
   - Set up advance inventory tracking for `garden6`, `garden9`, `garden10`, and `watches5` beginning in Q3. Historical data reveals massive concentration of sales volume during November and December.
3. **Consoles and Gaming Portfolio Trajectory:**
   - Monitor `consoles1` and `consoles2` viability. With quantities falling to single digits (1 to 6 units per month in mid-2018) and low revenue contribution, assess whether these listings warrant continued merchandising support.
